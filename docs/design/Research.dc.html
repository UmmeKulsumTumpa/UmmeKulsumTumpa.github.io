Research

At BARTA Research Lab, IIT, University of Dhaka, I work on NLP, large language models and AI agents: LLM-based analysis of health discussions on social media, cross-lingual evaluation of LLM agents in Bangla, and, for my M.Sc. thesis, adaptive inference for efficient LLMs.
