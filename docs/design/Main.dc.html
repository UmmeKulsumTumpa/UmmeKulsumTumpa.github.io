I'm a software engineer at Cefalo Bangladesh and a thesis-based M.Sc. student in Software Engineering at IIT, University of Dhaka, where I also completed my B.Sc.

At Cefalo, I've delivered production features across four projects (two in-house, two for clients), covering backend services, web applications (React, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript) and cross-platform mobile apps (Flutter, Firebase).

I practise spec-driven, agent-assisted development. LLM agents are part of my everyday engineering, from turning requirements into specs through implementation and code review. I build custom skills, hooks and plugins, and design the workflows that keep their output reliable.

As a researcher at BARTA Research Lab, IIT-DU, I work on NLP, large language models and AI agents, including LLM-based analysis of health discussions on social media and cross-lingual evaluation of LLM agents in Bangla. My M.Sc. thesis focuses on adaptive inference for efficient LLMs.
